A note on the curves

Why the attractors?

I like dynamical systems. A trajectory shows how a state changes under a rule, and I find the reverse question just as interesting: from observations of the motion, what can we learn about the rule?

That question connects several projects here. My SINDy work looks for differential equations in noisy, incomplete trajectories. My world-model work predicts future visual states from observations and actions. An autoregressive language model learns a distribution over the next token given its history; I think of generation as a learned, stochastic evolution of a sequence.

The attractors are a small expression of that interest. Here we know the equations and draw their trajectories. In the experiments, the question is what we can learn when the rule is unknown.
