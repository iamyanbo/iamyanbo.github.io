Why attractors? ↗

A different perspective.

Some of my favourite ideas in mathematics come from looking at a familiar problem differently. Fourier analysis expresses a complicated signal as a sum of sine waves, complex exponentials turn rotations into multiplication, and dual spaces turn our attention from vectors to the linear functions acting on them.

I see dynamical systems as another perspective on neural networks. That interest also led to my Reservoir Computing + SINDy project.
