2.2 · Image pyramid

All 18 assignment images use the same pyramid search. First images are convoluted with a Laplacian filter to emphasize edges (Bells and Whistles). Then the pyramid is built by downsampling by a factor of 2 until the smallest sample is around 100 pixels. The pyramid search starts at the smallest level, and the best offset is propagated to the next level up. At each level, a search window of 15 pixels is used to determine best alignment.

Examples where L2 fails

Raw-pixel L2 misaligns 01269v and 01725u. In both, dark areas/areas with clustered features provide easy low-error matches, causing the algorithm to misalign. This motivates the edge-enhancement step described in Bells and Whistles.
